Data Scientist - AI/ML

- Research and implement state-of-the-art ML algorithms

- Machine Learning and Deep Learning

- SQL and data manipulation
- Statistical analysis
- Data visualization (Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly)
- NLP techniques
- Model deployment and MLOps

Preferred Skills:
- Computer Vision (OpenCV, CNN)
- Big Data tools (Spark, Hadoop)
- Cloud ML services (AWS SageMaker, Azure ML)
- Docker and Kubernetes
- A/B testing and experimentation
- Research publications or Kaggle experience

Qualifications:

- 3+ years of hands-on ML/DS experience
- Strong mathematical and statistical foundation
- Experience with large-scale datasets
- Proven track record of delivering ML projects

Location: Bangalore / Hyderabad
